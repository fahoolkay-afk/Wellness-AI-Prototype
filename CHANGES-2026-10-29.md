### v1.2: website booking and sealed patient forms (docs/INTEGRATION-CONTRACT.md section 12)
- **Website booking page** `/book/<clinic>` (bridge clinics only) and `POST /public/book/<clinic>`: first name, mobile number, kind of visit, up to three preferred times, "been here before", and the text tick. Nothing else is accepted (no last name, birth date, reason or free text; a honeypot field; 10 per address per hour, 2 per phone per day). The tick is the contact's SMS consent, kept with the wording's version (`web-text-v1`). A staff note goes in the person's thread; it opens no reply window. The request reaches the records system as `booking.requested` with `channel: "web"`, `service`, `returning`, `textConsent`.
- **Sealed patient form** `/f/<token>`: the records laptop sends its public key (`PUT /bridge/v1/form-key`) and asks for one-time links (`POST /bridge/v1/forms`, at most 14 days; `/forms/revoke`). The page encrypts the answers in the patient's browser (ECDH P-256, HKDF-SHA-256, AES-256-GCM, AAD bound to the form and key) and sends only `{ v, keyId, epk, iv, ct }`. The link works once; the token is kept only as a hash. The front desk queues `form.submitted` with the ciphertext; it cannot open it.
- Released messages may link to the front desk's own `/f/<token>` path and nothing else on its address.
- Settings shows the website booking link for bridge clinics.
- Tests: `test/bridge-forms.test.ts` (website booking fields, refusals and limits; key, link, single use, ciphertext only in the cloud, only the laptop's key opens it, bound to its form; form links allowed only on the own path). Older bridge test updated for the three new request fields. Suite: 110 pass.
