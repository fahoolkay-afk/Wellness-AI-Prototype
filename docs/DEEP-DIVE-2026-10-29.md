## 1. Summary

1. The front desk inbox, AI tool loop and booking engine work for chat inside an open conversation. Bursts, phone-format merging, timezones and provider failures are handled well.
2. **The front desk cannot send a WhatsApp template.** Every WhatsApp message is free text (`src/channels/meta.ts:72`). Outside Meta's 24-hour window it either fails or silently switches to SMS. This is exactly the limit your friend described.
3. **The EHRM sends nothing.** "Release" only changes a status (`server.mjs:3063-3070`). There is no outbound code, no channel, no schedule, no delivery status.
4. The EHR-connected edition of the front desk is built backwards from your decision: it expects the EHRM to accept inbound calls, creates EHR patients from every sender (including spam), pushes message bodies into the chart, and runs its own reminders.
5. **Default settings let anyone forge a staff login** (default `SESSION_SECRET`, `src/config.ts:19`), and webhooks are unauthenticated unless `NODE_ENV=production`. Do not put real data on it yet.
6. Consent is too thin on both sides: front desk has one SMS STOP flag; EHRM demands email consent for every message, ignores SMS consent and has no WhatsApp consent.
7. Patient-facing wording: the EHRM outreach guard misses "cure" and "treat"; the front desk has no guard at all and its seed data says "Dr." and "diagnosis".
8. The integration design (laptop calls out, signed with an Ed25519 key, cloud holds a queue) is sound and fits the EHRM's walls. It is about 20 small build steps.
9. Build reminders (24 h / 2 h with confirm/reschedule) first; they pay for everything else. The year-end benefits campaign has a ~10 November deadline.
10. The UI should adopt the EHRM v1.2 design language; the front desk today is not keyboard-usable and its main colour fails contrast.

Direction of each flow: availability, catalog, appointment changes and released messages go **up** (push). Booking/cancel/reschedule requests, opt-outs, delivery receipts and message logs come **down** when the laptop polls `inbox`. If the laptop is asleep the cloud keeps answering from the last snapshot but only takes *requests*, never confirmed bookings.

## 3. What works

Front desk [RUN]:
- Three texts in 1.5 s get one reply; `+1 (416)…`, `4165557001` and `+1416…` land on one contact; WhatsApp and SMS merge on phone.
- STOP over SMS blocks later SMS; Meta error 131047 is mapped to a readable error and the thread is flagged.
- Provider failures (e.g. a Twilio 502 HTML page) mark the message failed and flag the thread; nothing crashes.
- Business-hour slots across the 1 Nov 2026 clock change are correct; a rescheduled appointment gets a fresh reminder.
- Same phone texting two clinics gives two separate contacts.
- Email AI drafts approve/edit/send correctly. Staff UI inserts text as text nodes (XSS-safe).
- Webhook signature checks pass tests and production refuses unsigned webhooks.

EHRM [RUN]:
- `bookingCheck` refuses past times, clashes, double-booked patients, and out-of-hours without a typed reason (409 with a readable message).
- Consent checks on outreach return 409 with what is missing; wording guard rejects "AI assistant", "osteopath", "A.I.".
- No send/sync routes exist (test X2 pins `/api/send`, `/api/messages`, `/api/sms`, `/api/webhooks` as 404).
- Kiosk token cannot be used as a session; foreign `Origin` gets 403; phone and email are encrypted at rest; audit hash chain verifies and is append-only.
- Policy layer denies by default and boot fails if a data kind has no rule (CP9).

## 5. WhatsApp and Meta: what's allowed [WEB unless noted]

- **24-hour window.** Free-form replies only within 24 h of the patient's last message (72 h after a click-to-WhatsApp ad). Outside it: approved templates only. Your friend is right, but only for messages *you start*; replies inside the window can be free text, from a person or the AI.
- **Template categories.** Utility = factual, user-expected (confirmation, reminder, reschedule/cancel, intake link, receipt). Marketing = anything promotional or "come back" (30-day check-in, recall, re-engagement, reviews, referrals, benefits reminder). Meta reclassifies mixed templates as marketing. Post-visit "how are you feeling?" is borderline; treat it as marketing unless the patient asked for follow-ups.
- **Pricing changed 2026-10-01.** Per delivered template; free-form service replies now cost after 1,000/number/month; utility templates in an open window are charged again. North America roughly: utility $0.0034, marketing $0.025 (third-party tables; not checked against Meta's card).
- **US marketing pause.** Marketing templates to US numbers have failed (error 131049) since 2025-04-01; still reported in force mid-2026. Unconfirmed whether Canadian +1 numbers are affected; test with one real Canadian number before relying on it.
- **Opt-in** is required, per category is recommended, and STOP / the opt-out button must be honoured.
- **AI bots.** From 2026-01-15 general-purpose chatbots are banned on the Business API; a clinic's booking/support receptionist is allowed.
- **Health content.** Meta signs no BAA/service-provider agreement. Keep WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram free of clinical content. Reminders say "your appointment", never the service name if it hints at a condition.
- **Messenger/Instagram.** 24 h window; `HUMAN_AGENT` tag extends to 7 days only for messages a person types. Treat both as **reply-only** channels; no funnel steps.
- **SMS in Canada (CASL).** Transactional messages (reminders) are exempt from consent but should still identify the clinic and keep STOP working. Marketing needs express consent or implied consent: 2 years from last visit, 6 months from an inquiry. Opt-outs within 10 business days; prove when consent was obtained. US-bound SMS needs 10DLC registration.

## 6. Funnels

### Rules that apply to all of them
- **Two lanes.** Lane U (utility/transactional): confirmations, reminders, no-show, waitlist. Lane M (marketing): check-ins, recall, re-engagement, reviews, referral, benefits, birthday. Each message carries three separate labels: EHRM class (care/commercial), CASL basis (exempt/implied-visit/implied-inquiry/express, with expiry), Meta category (utility/marketing). Note: EHRM's `check_48h` is "care" but likely Meta marketing.
- **Channel order.** Lane U: free-form WhatsApp if window open → WhatsApp utility template (with WhatsApp opt-in) → SMS (with SMS consent) → email. Lane M: email → SMS → WhatsApp marketing template only with WhatsApp marketing opt-in and a Canadian number.
- **Quiet hours (recipient's time).** Lane U 08:00-21:00. Lane M 10:00-19:00 Mon-Sat, never Sundays or Ontario statutory holidays. Checked when scheduled and again just before sending.
- **Caps.** Lane U ≤ 3 per appointment. Lane M ≤ 1 per 7 days and 2 per 30 days, none while a booking is upcoming or within 14 days of a cancellation, one lane-M funnel per person at a time, stop WhatsApp marketing after 2 unanswered.
- **Exit on booking, STOP, or a person stepping in**, always.
- **Guard after merge**: wording + clinical + cure/treat runs on the final text (a name can contain "Dr.").

## 7. Integration plan

**Decided:** the laptop never accepts inbound connections; it calls out. The EHRM owns consent, templates, release and reminders; the cloud delivers.

**Protocol ("EHR link v2")**
- **Pairing.** Owner (real mode, second step) enters a one-time code from the cloud admin screen. Laptop makes an Ed25519 keypair (`node:crypto`, zero deps), wraps the private key with the at-rest key, sends the public key; pins the cloud's key in return. On the EHRM it is a `devices` row of kind `sync` with one capability `sync.exchange` (W6), revocable from either side.
- **Every request and response signed**: `method\npath\nts\nnonce\nsha256(body)`; 5-minute skew, 10-minute nonce memory.
- **Endpoints (cloud side, laptop calls):** `pair`, `heartbeat`, `catalog` (practitioner display names, service `patient_label`s, hours, policy, `ai_policy` — all guarded on the laptop first), `availability` (21-day snapshot, every 2-5 min and after any local change), `appointments` (versioned upserts), `contacts` (minimum necessary only), `outbox` (signed template key+version+hash, channel order, variables, `send_after`, `expires_at`, idempotency key), `outbox/revoke`, `inbox` (requests, opt-outs, receipts, comms) and `inbox/ack`.
- **Idempotency:** laptop sends from a `sync_seq` cursor; cloud events have UUIDs; laptop stores them in `sync_inbox` and acks only after commit.
- **Laptop decides conflicts:** booking requests re-run `openSlots` + `bookingCheck`; clashes return `declined` with 3 alternates. Desk bookings always win.
- **Requests get their own table**, not a new appointment status. *Contradiction resolved:* the funnel pass suggested a 'requested' status; the integration pass is right, because `openSlots` (`server.mjs:3116`), `bookingCheck` (1677-1679) and the kiosk (3629) treat any non-cancelled row as real.
- **Nothing clinical travels.** Remove `notes` from the AI `book_appointment` tool in EHR mode (`src/ai/tools.ts:60`); cancel reasons travel as fixed codes only.
- **Message bodies in the cloud:** purge 30 days after the laptop acknowledges (0 days for template-only clinics); the laptop's encrypted `comms` table is the record.
- **Opt-outs** are enforced in the cloud immediately (even if the laptop is offline) and written to `consent_events` on the laptop later. Withdrawals win (C6).

**Build steps** (E = EHRM, F = front desk; each with a test; EHRM keeps zero deps and never edits frozen HTML)

P0 — safety first
1. F3 security: refuse default `SESSION_SECRET`/password, per-user accounts, clinic-scope every `:id` route, timing-safe compares, require webhook signatures always.

4. F2 EHR-mode lockdown: per-clinic edition; off: timer automations, broadcasts, auto patient creation, chart logging, `notes`; "cancel" becomes a request, not STOP.

P1 — link skeleton (data out only)
5. F4 `/link/v2/pair` + signed-request verifier. 6. E2 schema (`sync_*` tables), device kind `sync`, POLICY kinds. 7. E3 outbound worker (off by default) pushing heartbeat/catalog/availability; update C10, C13, CP2 and information-practices text. 8. F5 `EhrLinkBackend` reading the snapshot, soft holds, offline "requests only".

P2 — requests in
