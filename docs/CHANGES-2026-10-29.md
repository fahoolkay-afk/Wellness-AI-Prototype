## Decisions applied today

- **No SMS fallback without consent.** A reminder goes only on a channel where the patient agreed to
  reminders. If none is open, staff see "skipped" with the reason.
- **CANCEL.** Carriers treat a text of just "CANCEL" (also STOP, END, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE, STOPALL,
  REVOKE, OPTOUT, ARRET) as "stop texting me". We honour that, create an urgent call-back task in
  case they meant their appointment, and reminders now say "Reply C to confirm or CHANGE to
  reschedule". A sentence like "I need to cancel my appointment" goes to the receptionist as normal.
- **Wording and privacy are two separate checks on every outgoing message**, so they never conflict:
  the wording check (RHPA titles and claims) and the privacy check (PHIPA s.30, minimum necessary).

## Staff app redesign (Design OS v2)

The staff app (`public/`) was rebuilt on [DESIGN-OS-v2.md](DESIGN-OS-v2.md): the clinic teal and navy
of the records system, light and dark themes, reduced motion, 40px minimum targets, no `transition: all`.

- **Sign-in:** username and passphrase, then a phone-app code. First sign-in walks through choosing a
  passphrase and setting up the phone app. Being signed out after 15 idle minutes says so.
- **Roles:** each person sees only the screens their role allows (owner, front desk, practitioner, auditor).
- **Inbox:** who wrote each message is always shown (patient, staff name, or assistant with a sparkle
  mark); assistant drafts have a dashed outline and Approve & send; call summaries are marked as
  unverified; the composer explains when it can't send (patient said stop, WhatsApp 24-hour window closed).
- **Patient panel:** consent per channel (reminders and marketing), changes to confirm (phone or email
  proposed by the assistant or the records system), appointments, tasks, export (FHIR) and erase.
  Phone and email can't be edited inline; changing them is a deliberate step with a warning.
- **Funnels:** each one shows its trigger, steps and message as a preview, and whether it's a reminder
  (service consent) or marketing (express consent).
- **Campaigns:** preview who gets it and who's left out, then send with a confirmation tied to that exact preview.
- **Team, Audit, Settings:** add people (temporary passphrase shown once), reset or disable sign-in;
  audit log with chain status and FHIR export; processor agreements, wording rules, audience tags,
  retention and quiet hours.
- **Keyboard:** `j`/`k`, `/`, `r`, `e`, `.`, `g` then a letter, `?`.

The browser demo (`npm run demo:build`) runs the same code. Its crypto shim uses clearly labelled
stand-ins for AES-GCM and scrypt because the demo holds only sample data in memory.

## Second security pass (adversarial review of the fixes)

An independent reviewer tried to break the fixes above and proved 11 problems with working exploits.
All 11 are fixed, and each exploit is now a regression test in `test/security-regressions.test.ts`.

| # | Severity | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | High | Wrong phone-app codes were never limited | The code step counts toward the 5-try lockout and the 20-per-15-minutes address limit; locking ends half-finished sign-ins |
| B2 | Medium | A signed-in session could switch off phone-app codes | Moving codes to a new phone needs the passphrase; the old phone works until the new one is confirmed |
| B3 | High | An owner could reset someone who also works at another clinic and take over that clinic | Reset and disable only when you manage every clinic the person works at |
| B4 | Medium | One campaign confirmation could send several times | Each confirmation works once; one campaign at a time per clinic |
| B5 | High | STOP covered only one of two patient records sharing a number | STOP, START and unsubscribe apply to the number or address itself |
| B6 | Medium | Consent carried over to a new phone number or email | Each "yes" is tied to the address it was given for |
| B7 | Medium | Event funnels (new tag, booking) ignored quiet hours | They wait and send at the first check after quiet hours |
| B8 | High | A forged email header could make the sender look verified | Email never proves identity; one-time codes are used instead |
| B9 | Medium | An unreadable phone-app secret became a guessable one | Fails closed |
| B10 | Medium | Task titles and "needs reply" reasons held health text unencrypted and survived erase | Encrypted and bound to the patient; erase and retention clear them, plus proposals and old drafts |
| G1 | Low | The privacy check missed "physiotherapist", "chiro", plurals | Word stems and practitioner-title words |

Also from the review: marketing on WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram now carries "Reply STOP to opt out."
(CASL s.11). A staff message to someone who last wrote more than 24 hours ago (social) or 7 days ago
(text, email) counts as the clinic starting contact. The live-update stream stops within 5 seconds of
sign-out. `/healthz` re-checks the audit chain at most every 5 minutes.

Known limits, not fixed:
- Someone with direct write access to the database could delete the newest audit rows unnoticed. The
  records system now keeps a sealed copy of its audit "head"; the front desk should do the same.
- Encryption of tasks, appointments and calls is bound to the patient or clinic, not to the exact row.
- The export doesn't include tasks or staff notes yet.
- Reading the conversation list, pipeline and task list isn't written to the audit log; opening a
  conversation is.
